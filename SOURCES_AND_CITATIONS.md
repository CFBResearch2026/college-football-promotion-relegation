## Citation policy

This bibliography identifies authoritative or relevant source portals for the research. **Listing a source does not establish that a particular repository file was extracted from it.** For any public claim, identify the exact season-specific page or data extract and its retrieval date. Where a dataset's origin has not been established, mark it **unverified** rather than attributing it to a plausible provider.

## Required provenance fields for each source dataset

1. Repository filename and applicable seasons/classification.
2. Original publisher, dataset/page title, and direct source URL.
3. Date retrieved and any archived snapshot or version identifier.
4. Whether data were directly downloaded, transcribed, scraped, inferred, or merged.
5. Cleaning rules, team-name crosswalks, classification changes, deduplication, and excluded games.
6. Completeness and reconciliation results, including known missing or disputed records.
7. Applicable licensing, terms of use, and permission to redistribute the source data.
8. Derived outputs dependent on the dataset and their reproducible transformation steps.

## Important limitations

- The 2026 season is ongoing; records and coverage are incomplete.
- Model rankings and proposed conference assignments are **not** official NCAA ratings, policy, or approved restructuring.
- Cross-division comparisons and strength-of-schedule calibration are provisional.
- The presence of a dataset in a public repository does **not** establish redistribution rights. Review the applicable provider's terms and replace or remove restricted raw extracts as necessary.
- Canadian university programs are excluded from U.S. promotion/relegation tiers; any cross-border games used for strength of schedule require separate calibration and source verification.

## For reviewers

Please open a GitHub issue identifying the affected filename, season, team, original source URL, and suggested correction. Proposed corrections should distinguish confirmed source records from assumptions or modeling choices.
