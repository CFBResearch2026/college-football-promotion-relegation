PHASE 4: INITIAL BROADCAST INVENTORY AND MEDIA VALUATION SCREEN — 2026-10-17

Scope: 137 user-defined schools across 10 custom competitive conferences, NOT existing NCAA conferences. This is a transparent feasibility SCREEN, not a market forecast, appraisal, verified bid, or financeable commitment.

Existing audience pilot: 67 rows (62 from 2025, five from 2024). Imported 2025 pilot not independently rechecked. No full population, no random sample, no adequate lower-tier coverage. Do not regress media dollars on this biased sample.
Schedule: assume 12 regular-season games per school, 8 same-tier and 4 adjacent-tier. 137*12/2=822 games; 548 same-tier game-equivalents and 274 cross-tier game-equivalents. This is a bookkeeping inventory, NOT a constructed feasible schedule. 13-team levels and boundary-tier adjacency may require uneven assignments, byes, and schedule redesign. No cross-level games beyond adjacent levels are assumed.
Rights valuation: games placed in mutually exclusive 36 premium, 120 national secondary, 300 cable/streaming, 366 long-tail packages (822 total). Assumed LOW/BASE/HIGH gross $M/game: premium 12/18/25, national secondary 3/5/8, cable/streaming 0.8/1.5/2.5, long tail 0.12/0.3/0.6. THESE PRICES ARE INVENTED SCENARIO INPUTS, NOT OBSERVED RIGHTS PRICES. Segment selection not derived from verified matchup audiences.
Postseason: base cases assign zero incremental rights, because CFP exclusivity and current agreements constrain monetization. Two additional what-if cases assign $350M and $750M of new postseason rights WITHOUT claiming those rights are legally available. Never add existing $1.3B CFP revenue as though it is new project income.
