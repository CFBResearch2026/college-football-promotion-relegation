CFB 137-SCHOOL FINANCIAL RESEARCH — BASELINE RELEASE (2026-10-16)

Scope: 137 institutions in the user's proposed 10-conference alignment. Proposed conference membership is NOT historical membership.

Coverage: 137 of 137 have a reported FY2025/2024-25 athletics revenue figure. 117 Knight-Newhouse public-institution observations; 20 EADA observations for schools absent from the Knight-Newhouse crosswalk. 126 of 137 have reported athletics expenses in the merged dataset.

CRITICAL LIMITATIONS:
1. Knight-Newhouse and EADA use differing definitions, accounting and coverage; totals must not be treated as a harmonized audited 137-school sum.
2. Institutional support and student fees are included in reported athletics revenue where applicable; revenue minus expenses is not independent commercial profitability.
3. Knight-Newhouse combined conference/NCAA/media/postseason receipts are NOT football-only television rights or contractually at-risk money.
4. FY2025 individual NCAA media, conference, NCAA and postseason categories are NOT completely independently verified for the 20 EADA institutions.
5. Individual school conference payment values are limited reported figures and may include non-media distributions. Do not double count them with combined receipts.
6. Notre Dame ~$50m/year NBC figure is third-party estimate, NOT confirmed annual contract consideration; term reported through 2029 season.
7. No school-specific contractually protected amount or rights-at-risk amount has been verified. All transition/replacement payout scenarios remain illustrative.
8. Research request emails were sent by the user; replies and any attached financial reports have not been incorporated.
